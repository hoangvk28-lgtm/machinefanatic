# Grinders, routers, planers, joiners, nailers, heat guns, rotary tools, polishers, batteries
- Angle grinders: disc size 4-1/2 in (most common) vs 5 in vs 7 in; amps 7.5 to 15; no-load RPM 8,000 to 11,000; paddle vs slide switch; guard, side handle, vibration, dead-man (no-lock) switch; wheel rated RPM must be at or above tool RPM; kickback; brushless cordless on 18V/20V/M18 with 4-1/2 in; use eye, face, hearing protection; never remove guard.
- Routers: fixed vs plunge; 1/4 and 1/2 in collets; 1-1/2 to 3-1/4 HP; variable speed 8,000 to 25,000 RPM for large bits; soft start; depth adjustment; trim (palm) routers are 1 HP-class with 1/4 in collet for edges, laminates and hinges; dust collection; collet quality.
- Electric hand planers: 3-1/4 in standard width; amps 5 to 7; 16,000 to 20,000 cuts per minute; depth 1/16 to 1/8 in; rabbeting; bevel fence; helical vs straight blade; chip ejection direction.
- Biscuit joiners: #0, #10, #20 biscuits; 4 in blade; fence angle 0 to 90; Lamello and Porter-Cable; alignment; swelling biscuits; Domino alternatives.
- Brad nailers: 18 gauge, 5/8 to 2 in nails; pneumatic vs cordless (battery with flywheel/gas); depth adjustment; no-mar tip; jam clearing; air compressor requirement 0.3 to 1 CFM at 90 PSI.
- Heat guns: 1,200 to 1,800 W; dual temp (about 570 and 1,000 F); variable up to 1,200 F; for paint stripping, shrink wrap, thawing; lead paint risk; fire risk; nozzle attachments.
- Rotary tools: 5,000 to 35,000 RPM; variable speed; corded vs cordless; flex shaft; accessory shank 1/8 in; collet vs keyless chuck.
- Car polishers: dual-action (DA) vs rotary; orbit throw 8 to 21 mm; 5 to 6 in pads; speed settings; pad type; DA is safe for beginners; rotary cuts fast and burns paint.
- Batteries/chargers: Ah vs Wh; voltage naming (20V MAX = 18V nominal); platform lock-in; fast chargers and temperature; cell type 18650 vs 21700; compatibility adapters; storage at about 40 to 60 percent.
