# Oscillating multi-tools research
- Blade moves side to side through a small angle (about 2 to 4 degrees) at 10,000 to 20,000 OPM; a wider angle cuts faster but vibrates more. Makita quotes 3.6 degrees on the DTM52.
- Accessory interfaces: Starlock (Bosch and Starlock Plus/Max), universal-fit, and proprietary. Starlock transfers torque through a 3D profile with less slip than a clamp. Universal adapters fit older accessories.
- Cordless platforms: 12V suits flush cuts and detail; 18V/20V brushless suits sustained cutting. Bare tool vs kit changes cost.
- Vibration is the main fatigue factor; anti-vibration systems matter. Plunge cuts, flush cuts, scraping, grout and sanding are the use cases. Blades dull fast on nails; bimetal vs carbide.
- Corded tools give constant power for grout removal and long sessions.
