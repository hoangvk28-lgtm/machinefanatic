# Sanders research (random orbital, sheet, detail, belt, drywall)
- Random orbital: pad spins and orbits; orbit diameter (about 3/32 to 3/16 in) sets aggressiveness; 8,000 to 12,000 OPM typical. Swirl marks come from too-fast speed, skipping grits, or pressing hard. Hook-and-loop discs: 5-hole vs 8-hole patterns must match the pad for dust extraction. Vacuum hose on the port beats the onboard bag.
- Sand progression 80/120/180/220; skip no more than one grit step. Palm vs 6 in pro sanders. Pad dampening helps contour work.
- Cordless: brushless motors, 20V/18V, bare vs kit; battery adds weight.
- Sheet sanders: quarter sheet (1/4 sheet), half sheet; orbital (non-random) vibrate in small orbits, leave straight-line patterns across grain; good for corners, flat finishing, paint prep.
- Detail sanders: triangular pad, 11,000 to 14,000 OPM; corners; pad wear; accessory shapes.
- Belt sanders: 3x18, 3x21, 4x24 belts; fast stock removal; belt tracking; variable speed 900 to 1,500 FPM-ish; dust bag; sand with the grain; flush frame.
- Drywall sanders: long-reach with vacuum; 7 to 9 in heads; variable speed; dust containment is the point; 6 in brushless; use 120 to 220 grit; sponge pole alternative.
