# Band, scroll and track saws research
- Band saw size is the throat (wheel diameter, 8 to 10 in benchtop) plus resaw height under the guides (3-1/2 to 6 in on benchtop). Blade length is model specific (59-1/2 in on 9 in saws, 72 in on 10 in). Narrow blades turn tight curves; wide blades track straight and resaw.
- Induction motors run quieter than universal motors; benchtop saws are mostly 2.5 to 4.2 amp. Two-speed belts let you slow down for plastics and thin stock. Blade tracking and tension and guide setup matter more than motor size.
- Metal-cutting benchtop band saws (137 to 472 FPM variable speed, horizontal cut) are a different machine from wood band saws.
- Scroll saws: throat depth (16 to 22 in), stroke per minute range, arm design (C-arm vs parallel arm), tool-free blade clamps, table bevel. Parallel-arm and long-throat designs vibrate less; speed 400 to 1,600 SPM; pinned vs plain-end blades; blade tension and dust blower are the main ownership items.
- Track saws: depth on rail is lower than free-hand; rail play, splinter strip, 55 in rail too short for sheets; bare tool vs kit; corded vs cordless.
