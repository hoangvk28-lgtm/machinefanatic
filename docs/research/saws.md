# Circular, jig, reciprocating, miter and table saws research
- Circular saws: sidewinder (direct drive, lighter ~10 lb) vs worm drive (gear drive, ~14-15 lb, more torque, blade on the left, long handle). 7-1/4 inch blade standard, max depth ~2-9/16 inch at 90 degrees; 6-1/2 inch cordless is lighter. 15 amp corded; brushless cordless equals corded for framing, FLEXVOLT 60V.
- Bevel capacity (50-57 degrees), blade brake, magnesium shoe, rafter hook, dust blower, tool-free depth/bevel.
- Jigsaws: barrel grip vs top handle; orbital action settings; stroke length 3/4 to 1 inch; SPM; tool-free blade change T-shank; blade choice (TPI) matters more than motor; bevel to 45 degrees.
- Reciprocating saws: stroke length 1 to 1-1/8 inch, SPM 2,800 to 3,000+, orbital action, one-handed compact (Hackzall) vs full size (Sawzall). Blades: TPI for metal vs wood demolition.
- Miter saws: blade size 10 vs 12 inch, single vs dual bevel, sliding extends crosscut (about 12 inch non-sliding 2x8, sliding 14 to 16 inch), fence height, laser/shadow line, dust collection poor (~ half), blade quality (24T vs 60-80T).
- Table saws: portable jobsite 10 inch, rip capacity 24 to 32.5 inch, rack and pinion fence, riving knife, 15 amp, stand weight; flesh sensing brake exists only on SawStop. Dado capacity limited on portable.
