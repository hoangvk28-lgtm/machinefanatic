# Delivery reports

## 1. running-watts-vs-starting-watts
**Competitors studied:** Popular Mechanics, PlugCount, Electric Ninjas, Westinghouse owner documentation.
**3 unique angles added:** additional surge versus total starting watts; apparent power and power factor; overlapping automatic motor starts.
**Could not verify:** universal surge duration, universal motor multiplier, universal soft starter reduction.
**Counts:** metaTitle 31; metaDescription 131; body 2,794 words; 6 internal links.

## 2. what-size-generator-to-run-a-house
**Competitors studied:** Generac sizing guide, Home Depot worksheet, Lights On Lab house sizing guide.
**3 unique angles added:** outage priority tiers; automatic pump overlap; connection method as a capacity constraint.
**Could not verify:** one generator size based on square footage or a universal household wattage.
**Counts:** metaTitle 34; metaDescription 133; body 2,751 words; 6 internal links.

## 3. inverter-generator-vs-conventional-generator
**Competitors studied:** Consumer Reports, Honda Eco Throttle documentation, Generac inverter documentation, PlugCount comparison.

**Could not verify:** a universal noise advantage or fixed fuel saving for all inverter models.
**Counts:** metaTitle 29; metaDescription 143; body 2,510 words; 5 internal links.
